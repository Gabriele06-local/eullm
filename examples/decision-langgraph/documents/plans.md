# Plans and prices

Acme Notes has four plans: Free, Personal, Team and Enterprise. Every plan
keeps your notes in sync on all your devices.

The Free plan holds up to three notebooks and 500 notes, on two devices.

The Personal plan costs 4 euros a month, or 40 euros a year paid at once. It
has no limit on notebooks, notes or devices.

The Team plan costs 8 euros per seat a month and is billed once a year, for
at least three seats. Seats can be added at any time; a seat added during the
year is charged for the months left.

Enterprise is priced by quote, for organisations of more than 200 people that
need single sign-on or their own data retention rules.
