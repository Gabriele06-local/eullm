# Signing in and two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a code from an authenticator app after the
password. It can be switched on under Settings, Security.

When two-factor authentication is switched on, Acme Notes shows ten recovery
codes. Each one signs you in once without the authenticator app.

Without the phone and without a recovery code, support checks your identity
before switching two-factor authentication off. The check takes up to three
working days.

A password can be reset from the sign-in page; the link in the email works
for one hour.
