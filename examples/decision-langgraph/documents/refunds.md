# Refunds

A plan paid every month is not refunded, but it can be cancelled at any time:
it stays active until the end of the month already paid.

A plan paid once a year is refunded in full when the refund is asked for
within 30 days of the payment.

After those 30 days, a yearly payment is refunded for the full months left,
on request to billing@acme-notes.example.

A refund goes back to the card or account the payment came from, within ten
working days.
